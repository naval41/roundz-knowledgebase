## What "working" looks like for this batch

Read `14_Warmup_Dry_Run.md` Part 6 before judging results: **cold email into IT services runs ~3.5% reply rate, the lowest of any tracked industry.** On five contacts, the mathematically expected number of replies is **0.2**. Silence here is not a signal — it is the base case.

So judge Batch 2 on these instead:

1. **Did anyone engage with the fraud premise at all**, even to say "not a problem for us"? That tells you whether the Segment 2 narrative is landing or bouncing off.
2. **Compare against Batch 1's replies.** The question this batch exists to answer is *which segment is the beachhead*, not *did five emails convert*. That comparison needs both batches read side by side at the review gate.
3. **Did anyone ask price?** For this segment that's the single most valuable data point available — `13_Cold_Email_Launch_Plan.md` §2.4 flags that willingness-to-pay here is unknown and probably well below the product-company ACV. One unprompted "how much?" is worth more than three polite no's.
4. **Log a reason code on every rejection** (`NO_HIRING_NOW`, `TRUST_AI`, `PRICE`, `HAVE_VENDOR`) with a reopen trigger and a diarised date.

**Do not kill the staffing segment on five contacts' silence.** You would need roughly 150 to know anything statistically. Kill it or keep it based on what the replies *say* — and if two or three of these convert to paid pilots at any price, you have found a faster beachhead than the Series A–C product companies.
