These were an explicit decision made with the risks flagged beforehand. Recording them so the results get read correctly later:

1. **Emails were NOT bounce-verified first.** `13_Cold_Email_Launch_Plan.md` §1.5 requires NeverBounce/ZeroBounce before Batch 1 and treats >1 bounce as a stop-and-re-verify event. Apollo "verified" runs ~85-92% accurate, so expect roughly 2-4 bounces across 28.
2. **All 28 went out at once**, against the 8-12/day ramp and 25/day hard ceiling in §1.3.
3. **Identical body to all 28**, against the "never the same text to more than two people" rule in `14_Warmup_Dry_Run.md` Part 4B.
4. **Sent to the 1,000-5,000 "small elephant" segment**, which §2.3 predicts will not move on a founder-led timeline.
