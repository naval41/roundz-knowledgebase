# Founding Sales (Peter Kazanjy): Actionable Notes for Roundz

> Distilled from the full book (`Founding_Sales.pdf` / `.txt`, 475 pp).
> This is the reference we apply when building Roundz sales/marketing assets.

**Core thesis:** GTM has two epochs. (1) **Figure it out**: the founder personally sells
dozens of deals to prove the value is real, customers get value, and they'll pay again.
(2) **Scale it**: package that proven motion and teach it to hired reps. You cannot skip
epoch 1. Hiring a VP of Sales to "figure it out for you" is the #1 company-killer.
**Roundz is squarely in epoch 1.**

---

## 1. Mindset shifts (Ch.1)
1. **Plenty, not scarcity**: huge sea of prospects; no single "no" matters.
2. **Activity above all**: outcomes = quantity × quality of customer-facing activity.
3. **Be direct**: state your purpose; directness respects the buyer's time.
4. **Many shallow relationships**: touch many contacts broadly, not a few deeply.
5. **Assume the sale is inevitable**: confidence is contagious.
6. **Expect to win, unfazed by rejection**: even 35% win = 65% loss. Rejection is the job.
7. **Record everything, efficiently**: "if it's not in the CRM it didn't happen."
8. **Be expert & authoritative**: command of market/business begets fearlessness.
9. **Live in a glass house**: transparency (metrics, recorded calls, open pipeline) is an asset.

## 2. Narrative & product marketing (Ch.2)
**Cohesive narrative:** Problem/status-quo → agitate its cost → introduce solution (as a
category shift) → specifics/proof. Establish the problem *before* pitching yourself.
- **Quant proof** = hard ROI (metric X → Y%). **Qual proof** = testimonials/logos/stories.
  Early: sell the *logic* of value, instrument proof as customers succeed.
- **Pricing:** you MUST charge (proves the value exchange). Price off **value delivered**
  (ROI-anchored), not cost-plus. Favor cash: annual, up front, auto-renew.

## 3. Sales materials (Ch.3)
**Speed > polish.** Materials are living docs.
Need: **deck, cold-email templates, call/voicemail scripts, demo script, video, one-pager.**
**Deck as "chapters":** Title → Problem (agitate) → Solution (new paradigm) → How it works
(features → use case → business pain, never feature-listing) → Proof (ROI/logos/testimonials)
→ Pricing → Next steps/ask → **Appendix** (build a slide whenever a question recurs >once).
Slides are **object-oriented**: modular, reusable.

## 4. Prospecting / ICP (Ch.4)
- **ICP = abstracted demand-signifier metadata**: observable/searchable attributes that
  correlate with having your pain + means to buy (size, vertical, stack, funding, open roles, tools).
- **Rabbits/Deer/Elephants → hunt Deer** (right-sized, reliably closeable deals).
- **Account sourcing:** build target lists from LinkedIn/job boards/databases matching ICP.
- **Point-of-contact discovery:** find multiple champions per account (cascading contacts);
  engage economic buyer + user + influencers. Email high, get referred down (Cold Calling 2.0).
- **Qualify:** BANT / ANUM / ChAMP; prioritize clearest pain + buying ability.

## 5. Outreach & appointment setting (Ch.5)
**Cold email:** specific subject (never "Quick question") → personalized relevance hook →
one crisp problem/value line → proof (logo/metric) → **one low-friction CTA** (a 20-min call,
propose a time). Short, mobile-readable, one ask.
**Calling:** goal = set the appointment, not close. Pattern-interrupt + reason tied to pain;
be direct. **30-sec voicemail script** as part of cadence.
**Cadence:** multi-touch, multi-channel over ~2–3 weeks (email + call + voicemail + social);
many touches per contact; instrument and iterate.
**Referrals** convert best, ask customers/network; mine shared connections.

## 6. Inbound (Ch.6)
Inbound = highest intent ("bluebirds"). **Low-friction forms** (minimum fields).
**Speed-to-lead is everything**: respond in minutes (live chat). Lightweight qualifying
discovery; persistent cadenced follow-up on non-converters.

## 7. Pitching / demo / objections (Ch.7)
**Persuasion formula (multiplicative):**
`Sale = Potential Value × Value Comprehension × Belief`, a zero anywhere kills the deal.
- Default to **inside sales** (screen-share) for volume.
- **Pre-call planning:** research attendees/pains; set agenda + desired next step.
- **Structure:** rapport + **up-front contract** (agree agenda/time/that a decision results)
  → **discovery first** → mirror problem → **demo by sections** (feature→use case→business value)
  → comprehension check-ins / micro-yeses → live ROI math → **explicitly ask for the sale**.
- **Challenger:** teach, tailor, take control; respectfully challenge assumptions.
- **Objection loop:** acknowledge → clarify root → reframe/evidence → confirm → advance.
  Handle: Authority (enlist champion), Need (re-discover/quantify), Fear-of-change (de-risk:
  pilot/references), Timing (isolate; opportunity cost), Price (re-anchor on ROI, never lead
  with discount), Budget (find holder), Competition (know cold; differentiate on what THIS
  buyer cares about; don't trash-talk).
- Follow up with recap + next meeting booked before the call ends. **Drill & record your calls.**

## 8. Down-funnel (Ch.8)
- **Negotiate on terms, not just price. Trade, don't give** (concession → term/seats/cash/case study).
- Prefer **cash up front**; pay commission on cash received, not bookings.
- **Actively Close-Lost** dead deals; record *why*. Work **"close to the money"** first.
- **Pipeline stages:** Demo Scheduled → Qualified → Seeking Approval → Proposal → Negotiation
  → Verbal → Contract Sent → Won/Lost/Unqualified. Flag **opps untouched 30 days**.

## 9. Customer success (Ch.9)
CS = deliver the value promised in the sale → renewal. Under-investing is the #2 founder error.
- **Onboarding:** rediscover the goals that drove purchase; assign homework/micro-contracts;
  you can charge for implementation.
- **Support:** low-friction capture; diffuse frustration first; fast response; build an article
  when a question recurs (>once). (If one user complains, ~10× hit it silently.)
- **Proactive monitoring:** instrument usage; watch value precursors/outcomes; spend effort on
  movable C+/B- accounts. Capture outcomes in-product + 30-day/NPS check-ins.
- **QBRs:** quarterly, rediscovery, before/after KPIs, ROI delivered, remediation if needed,
  expansion (only if delivering). **Renewals:** auto-renew clause + card on file; still ask again.

## 10. Scaling & hiring (Ch.10–12)
- **Scale = add reps ("cylinders"), not existing reps selling more.**
- **When to hit the gas:** good B2B win rate = **15–30%** of first meetings → won.
  <10–15% → fix messaging/product/pricing first. >30% → raise price and/or scale.
- Maturity: founder-does-all → +SDR/CSM → +2 AEs → sales pod → clone pods.
- Hire sales at the **engineer quality bar**; screen with artifacts (written screen, voicemail
  homework, mock-presentation-sells-you). Hire in **cohorts**; run a **1–2 week boot camp**.

## 11. Rules of thumb (keep handy)
- Win rate good = **15–30%**; **cost of sales ≤20%** of revenue closed; a $100k rep books ~$500k.
- Manager span **6–9**; Sales Ops pays off ~10 reps.
- Support article / appendix slide when a question recurs **>once**.
- `Sale = Potential Value × Value Comprehension × Belief`.
- Pipeline: **untouched 30 days = to-do list**. "If there's doubt, there is no doubt" (hiring).

## How this maps to Roundz's problem ("emails aren't converting; GTM not cracked")
The book's diagnosis of our exact symptom:
1. **We're skipping discovery + narrative.** Cold emails that don't convert usually lead with
   product, not with the prospect's felt pain. Fix: narrative-first outreach (Problem → cost → CTA).
2. **ICP is too broad / not demand-signified.** "CTOs at 50–250" isn't searchable enough. We need
   observable triggers (just raised + hiring N engineers + remote + using HackerRank/Greenhouse).
3. **One-touch outreach.** We need a multi-touch, multi-channel cadence, instrumented.
4. **No proof yet → we must sell the value logic + offer a de-risked pilot (that we still price).**
5. **Founder must personally close the first ~dozen** before any rep/scaling talk.
