# Roundz

## The AI interview engine for the AI-native era.

**"Your screening tests if code runs. We test if the engineer does."**

---

### The Problem

Screening signal has collapsed. AI copilots make almost any candidate's code *run*, so a passing take-home or auto-graded challenge no longer tells you who can actually engineer. Meanwhile the job itself changed: the daily work is now *directing* AI well, not typing every line. Your current filters measure the wrong thing, and your best engineers burn hours interviewing people who never should have reached them.

### What Roundz Does

Roundz runs your **real technical interview loop** with a live voice AI interviewer. It watches the candidate code (Monaco) and design (Excalidraw whiteboard) in real time, probes their reasoning out loud, and produces an explainable, level-aware, rubric-scored report with a clear hire verdict. Roundz filters the funnel: **you meet only the pre-vetted top ~20% and make every final call.** That's the 80/20 model: let AI vet the rest, keep the human decision.
