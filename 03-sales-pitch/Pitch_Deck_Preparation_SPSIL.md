• **To Pitch?** Yes—to position [Roundz.ai](http://roundz.ai/) as the premier, **all-in-one AI preparation suite** that mirrors the rigorous standards of companies like Google, Amazon, and Meta

• **For what?** To provide a **scalable, 24/7 accessible alternative** to expensive human coaching (
100–300/hour) that helps candidates "level up" their career and master the "interview muscle" through repetition

**3. How do you get them to “Yes!” with your STORY?**

• **The Situation (The Current Landscape):** We are in an era where the AI interview market is exploding toward **$59.8 billion**, and employers are institutionalising AI-driven hiring

• **The Problem (The Struggle):** Even brilliant engineers are failing because they struggle to **articulate their thought process** or handle the "pressure of being observed"

• **The Solution (The Hero - [Roundz.ai](http://roundz.ai/)):** Introduce Roundz.ai’s **four-pillar setup** (Behavioural, Problem Solving, HLD, and LLD)

• **The Impact (The Transformation):** Describe the transformation from an anxious candidate to one who feels "cool as a cucumber"

• **The Lessons (The Call to Action):** Conclude with the lesson that **practice makes permanent**
