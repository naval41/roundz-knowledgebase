## PRODUCTION NOTES

| Element | Recommendation |
|---------|---------------|
| **Voiceover** | Founder's voice (authenticity > polish for PH). Confident, slightly fast-paced, technical tone. Not salesy. |
| **Music** | Minimal electronic/ambient. Tension in Act 1, resolves in Act 2, energy in Act 3. Drops out for CTA. |
| **Visuals** | 70% real product screen recordings, 30% motion graphics/text cards. PH audience respects authenticity. |
| **The AI voice clip** | Let the audience *hear* the AI ask one follow-up question at 1:18–1:22. This is the "wow moment" — voice AI selling itself. |
| **Pacing** | Fast cuts in Act 1 (problem), slower in Act 3 (product demo), punchy in Act 5 (close). |
| **Captions** | Add subtitles throughout — many PH users watch on mute initially. |
| **Total duration** | ~2 minutes 30 seconds (voiceover ~2:28, with 2s breathing room in text cards). |
