### 🔍 Product Overview

The platform is designed to automate the end-to-end hiring process:

* Third-party enterprises can post job roles based on their hiring needs.
* Candidates can browse available roles and apply for relevant positions.
* Recruiters review applications and shortlist suitable candidates.
* Shortlisted candidates are scheduled for interview loops.
* Interviews are conducted by AI-powered voice agents capable of evaluating candidate skills.
* The platform operates 24/7, and every candidate receives detailed feedback, addressing one of the biggest gaps in current hiring systems.

This creates a fully automated, AI-driven hiring loop.

---

### 🚀 Problems We Are Solving

1. High engineering and recruiter bandwidth required to screen candidates.
2. Long hiring cycles (currently ~30 days, with a goal to reduce to ~10 days).
3. Inefficient evaluation, where candidates are primarily assessed based on resumes instead of actual skills.

---

### 💡 Our Approach

* Instead of manually screening 100% of applicants, the platform uses AI interviews to evaluate candidates.
* Candidates are assessed based on real skills, not just resume data.
* Only the top ~5% of candidates are shortlisted and passed to hiring managers.
* Hiring managers are not removed from the decision-making process; instead, their effort is optimized by focusing only on high-quality candidates.
* This significantly reduces overall hiring bandwidth while improving quality and speed.
