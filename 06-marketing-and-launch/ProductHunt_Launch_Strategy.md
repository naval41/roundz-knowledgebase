# Roundz.ai — Product Hunt Launch Strategy

## 1. How Product Hunt Works

### The Basics
- Products launch at **12:01 AM PT** and compete for upvotes until midnight
- Top products get **"Product of the Day"** badge (massive credibility)
- Ranking algorithm weighs: **unique upvotes, comments, engagement quality, and recency**
- Products are discoverable in category pages, newsletters, and the daily digest email (sent to millions)
- A strong launch drives: signups, press coverage, investor attention, backlinks (SEO), and social proof

### The Algorithm (What Actually Matters)
1. **Unique upvotes from real PH accounts** — old accounts with history weigh more than new ones
2. **Comment quality and quantity** — genuine discussions rank higher than "Great product!"
3. **Engagement velocity** — steady stream throughout the day beats a spike-then-drop
4. **Hunter reputation** — if a well-known hunter submits your product, it gets more initial visibility
5. **Account authenticity** — PH actively penalizes vote manipulation and new burner accounts

## 3. Launch Day Execution

### Timing
- **Best day**: Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday (highest traffic)
- **Avoid**: Mondays (busy), Fridays (low engagement), weekends (low traffic)
- **Launch at**: 12:01 AM PT sharp
- **All-hands from**: 12:00 AM to 11:59 PM PT

### Hour-by-Hour Plan

**12:00 AM PT — Go Live**
- Submit product (or have Hunter submit it)
- Post your **Maker's Comment** immediately (see Section 5)
- Notify your team via Slack/WhatsApp: "We're live!"

**12:00–6:00 AM PT — Early Momentum**
- Send personal DMs to your strongest supporters (India time zone advantage — this is daytime IST)
- Ask them to: (1) Upvote, (2) Leave a genuine comment, (3) Share on social
- Post on X/Twitter with the PH link
- Do NOT say "please upvote" — say "we just launched, would love your feedback"

**6:00–9:00 AM PT — US Wakes Up**
- Send second wave of outreach (US contacts)
- Post on LinkedIn with a personal founder story
- Cross-post to relevant Slack communities (not spammy — add value)
- Reply to every single comment on PH within 10 minutes

**9:00 AM–12:00 PM PT — Peak Hours**
- This is when PH traffic peaks
- Post on Hacker News (Show HN)
- Send email blast to your mailing list
- Share in relevant subreddits (r/startups, r/SaaS, r/recruitinghell, r/cscareerquestions)
- Continue responding to every comment

**12:00–6:00 PM PT — Sustain Momentum**
- Second LinkedIn post (different angle, maybe a team member)
- Share on Dev.to or Hashnode
- DM third wave of supporters
- Keep commenting and engaging on PH

**6:00–11:59 PM PT — Final Push**
- Thank everyone who supported
- Share real-time results on social (screenshots of ranking)
- Final outreach to stragglers

### Critical Rules
1. **NEVER ask for upvotes directly** — PH penalizes this. Say "check it out" or "would love your feedback"
2. **NEVER send PH direct links in mass emails** — PH detects referral patterns and demotes
3. **Reply to every comment** — engagement rate matters
4. **Keep comments substantive** — "Great product!" comments from new accounts hurt you
5. **Don't buy votes** — PH has sophisticated fraud detection, you will get banned

## 6. Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Why It Kills Your Launch |
|---------|------------------------|
| Launching on a Monday/Friday | Low traffic, compete with fewer eyeballs |
| Mass email with direct PH link | PH detects referral patterns, penalizes you |
| Asking for "upvotes" | Against TOS, flagged by community |
| Not replying to comments | Kills engagement score, looks abandoned |
| Generic gallery images | First image is everything — must be compelling |
| No product video | Video products get 2-3x more engagement |
| Launching without a pre-built audience | Day-of outreach alone rarely wins PotD |
| Only having new PH accounts support you | Votes from new/inactive accounts are heavily discounted |
| Launching an incomplete product | PH community is brutal on half-baked products |
| Not having a clear, differentiated tagline | "AI hiring tool" blends in with 100 others |

## 7. Strategies Specific to AI/HR-Tech Products

### Hook Into the "Anti-AI" Narrative
The tech community is divided on AI in hiring. Use this tension:
- Frame as "AI that makes hiring MORE human" (saves time for meaningful human conversations)
- Emphasize: "Human-in-the-loop — AI augments, never replaces"
- Lead with the anti-cheating angle — honest candidates love this

### Target Engineering Communities
AI hiring tools sell to CTOs, not HR. Focus outreach on:
- Engineering leadership Slack communities
- CTO/VP of Eng forums
- Y Combinator community (if applicable)
- Developer Twitter/X (especially those who complain about interviewing)

### Offer an Irresistible Launch Deal
- "First 50 PH users: 3 free candidate screenings" (employer side)
- "PH community: free DSA mock interview" (candidate side — drives traffic)
- Time-limited to create urgency

### Leverage the "$555K" Number
Your strongest hook for virality. Use it in:
- First gallery image
- Social media posts
- Maker comment
- Every piece of content

## 10. Reference: PluralAI Product Hunt Launch Analysis

PluralAI (AI interview platform, similar space) launched on Product Hunt with:
- Focus on AI-powered technical assessments
- Clean product screenshots showing the interview interface
- Strong maker engagement in comments
- Positioning as a "fairer" alternative to traditional coding tests

**What to learn from them:**
- They positioned as candidate-friendly (reducing interview anxiety)
- Their gallery showed the actual product in action
- They had active community engagement

**Where Roundz can differentiate:**
- Voice AI (PluralAI doesn't do real-time voice conversations)
- Anti-cheat/proctoring (unique differentiator)
- The full hiring loop (not just screening — from JD to hire recommendation)
- The $555K cost data (more compelling than generic "save time" messaging)
- The 80/20 model (specific, memorable framework)
